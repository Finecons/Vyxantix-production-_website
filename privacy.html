Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At VyxAntix ("we", "our", or "us"), protecting your privacy and business data is fundamental to everything we design and build. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website, cloud ERP services, and related applications.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, request a demo, fill out forms, or interact with our customer support. This includes your name, business email address, phone number, company name, industry, team size, and billing information.

2. Your Business Data & Ownership

You own 100% of your business data. All accounting transactions, invoices, customer records, inventory levels, and payroll files entered into your VyxAntix account belong exclusively to you. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your business records.

3. Hosting, Security & Backups

All data is securely hosted on high-availability infrastructure located within India, compliant with Indian statutory requirements. We employ industry-standard 256-bit AES encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Automated encrypted backups are performed daily and retained for 30 days.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@vyxantix.in.
